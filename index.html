About Redmond Carpentry

Building With Precision in Monroe, CT

Redmond Carpentry, LLC is a specialized residential remodeling and carpentry firm serving homeowners throughout Monroe and Fairfield County, Connecticut.

We believe that fine carpentry requires a combination of structural discipline, quality wood selection, clean joinery, and respect for the homeowner's living space. Whether constructed outdoors under changing weather conditions or inside an occupied home, our work is engineered for long-term stability and aesthetic beauty.
